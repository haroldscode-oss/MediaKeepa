Filing a DMCA Takedown Notice

If you believe that content accessible through our service infringes your copyright, you may send a written notice to our DMCA Agent listed above. Your notice must include the following information as required by the DMCA (17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3)):

  1. Your signature: A physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner or person authorized to act on their behalf.
  2. Identification of the copyrighted work: A description of the copyrighted work that you claim has been infringed. If multiple works are covered by a single notification, you may provide a representative list.
  3. Identification of infringing material: A description of where the material that you claim is infringing is located on our service (please provide the specific URL or detailed description so we can locate it).
  4. Your contact information: Your address, telephone number, and email address so we can contact you.
  5. Good faith statement: A statement that you have a good faith belief that the use of the material in the manner complained of is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  6. Accuracy statement: A statement that the information in the notification is accurate, and under penalty of perjury, that you are authorized to act on behalf of the owner of an exclusive right that is allegedly infringed.

Our Response Process

Upon receiving a valid DMCA takedown notice that meets the requirements listed above, we will:

  • Review the notice within 48-72 hours
  • Take appropriate action, which may include:
    • Blocking access to the specific URL or content identified
    • Disabling download functionality for the reported content
    • Removing cached or temporary files
  • Notify you of the action taken
  • Document the complaint and our response for our records

Counter-Notification

If you believe that content you posted was wrongly removed or disabled in response to a DMCA notice, you may file a counter-notification with our DMCA Agent. Your counter-notification must include:

  1. Your physical or electronic signature
  2. Identification of the material that was removed and its location before removal
  3. A statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good faith belief the material was removed by mistake or misidentification
  4. Your name, address, and telephone number
  5. A statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the Federal District Court for the judicial district in which your address is located (or if you are outside the United States, that you consent to jurisdiction of any judicial district in which the service provider may be found)
  6. A statement that you will accept service of process from the person who provided the original notification or an agent of such person

Repeat Infringer Policy

We have adopted a policy of terminating, in appropriate circumstances, access to our service by users who are repeat infringers. We may block IP addresses or take other technical measures to prevent repeat offenders from accessing our service.

Important Information

Please note: We are a neutral service provider that facilitates downloads of URLs provided by users. We do not host, store, or control any third-party content. We act solely as an intermediary tool.

False claims: Please be aware that under 17 U.S.C. § 512(f), you may be liable for damages (including costs and attorneys' fees) if you materially misrepresent that material is infringing your copyrights.
