# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** January 17, 2025

## 1. Introduction

MediaKeepa ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our Service.

**Key Points:**
- ✅ We collect minimal data (only what's needed for the service to function)
- ✅ We do NOT sell your data to third parties
- ✅ We do NOT track you for advertising purposes
- ✅ Downloaded files are automatically deleted after 1 hour
- ✅ You can request data deletion at any time

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide

When you use MediaKeepa, you provide:

- **URLs:** The web addresses of content you want to download (e.g., YouTube, TikTok, Instagram links)
- **Format preferences:** Selected download quality and format (video, audio, image)

We **do NOT require:**
- ❌ Email address or registration
- ❌ Payment information (service is free)
- ❌ Personal identification documents
- ❌ Social media login credentials

### 2.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you visit MediaKeepa, we automatically collect:

#### **Technical Information:**
- **IP Address:** Your internet connection's IP address (used for rate limiting and abuse prevention)
- **Browser Type:** Your web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, etc.)
- **Device Type:** Desktop, mobile, or tablet
- **Operating System:** Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS, Android
- **Timestamp:** Date and time of your visit

#### **Usage Information:**
- **URLs Submitted:** Which platforms you download from (to track platform compatibility)
- **Download Metadata:** File size, format, quality (to improve service)
- **Error Logs:** Failed downloads or technical issues (to fix bugs)

#### **Cookies and Local Storage:**
- **Theme Preference:** Dark mode or light mode setting (stored locally in your browser)
- **Rate Limiting Cookies:** To prevent abuse and ensure fair access

We do **NOT** collect:
- ❌ Your name, email, or contact information
- ❌ Downloaded file contents (files are deleted after 1 hour)
- ❌ Browsing history outside of MediaKeepa
- ❌ Location data (beyond country-level from IP)

We use collected information to:

- ✅ **Add platform support:** Prioritize new platforms based on user demand
- ✅ **Fix bugs:** Identify and resolve common errors
- ✅ **Update UI/UX:** Improve user interface based on usage patterns

### 3.3 Legal Compliance

We may use information to:

- ✅ **Respond to DMCA requests:** Comply with copyright takedown notices
- ✅ **Comply with law enforcement:** Respond to valid legal requests (subpoenas, court orders)
- ✅ **Prevent illegal activity:** Block users violating Terms of Service

We will **notify you** if legally required to disclose your information, unless prohibited by law.

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

### 5.1 Temporary File Storage

- **Downloaded files** are stored in `temp_downloads/` folder
- **Automatically deleted after 1 hour** (or when download completes)
- **NOT backed up or archived** to long-term storage

### 5.2 Log Retention

- **Usage logs** (URLs, IP addresses, timestamps) are retained for **30 days**
- **Error logs** are retained for **90 days** (to diagnose technical issues)
- **DMCA logs** are retained **indefinitely** (required for legal compliance)

### 5.3 Right to Deletion

You can request deletion of your data by emailing **privacy@mediakeepa.com**. We will:

- ✅ Delete all personal data within **30 days** (except where legally required to retain)
- ✅ Confirm deletion via email
- ✅ Provide a report of data deleted (upon request)

**Note:** We cannot delete data required for legal compliance (DMCA logs, active legal requests).
