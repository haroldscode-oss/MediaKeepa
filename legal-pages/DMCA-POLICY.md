# DMCA Policy

**Last Updated:** January 17, 2025

## Copyright Infringement Notification

MediaKeepa respects the intellectual property rights of others and expects users to do the same. In accordance with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA), we will respond promptly to notices of alleged copyright infringement.

## DMCA Agent

If you believe that your copyrighted work has been copied in a way that constitutes copyright infringement through our service, please provide our DMCA Agent with the following information:

**DMCA Agent Contact:**
- **Email:** dmca@mediakeepa.com
- **Response Time:** 24-48 hours

## Required Information for DMCA Takedown Notice

To file a valid DMCA notice, you must provide:

1. **Identification of the copyrighted work** claimed to have been infringed
2. **Identification of the infringing material** (specific URL or description)
3. **Your contact information** (name, address, email, phone number)
4. **A statement** that you have a good faith belief that use of the material is not authorized
5. **A statement of accuracy** under penalty of perjury that you are authorized to act on behalf of the copyright owner
6. **Your physical or electronic signature**

## How We Respond to DMCA Notices

Upon receiving a valid DMCA notice, we will:

1. **Acknowledge receipt** within 24-48 hours
2. **Review the claim** to determine validity
3. **Take appropriate action:**
   - Block access to the specific content/URL if hosted by us
   - Implement technical measures to prevent future access if necessary
   - Notify the user who submitted the content (if applicable)
4. **Document the request** for our records

## Important Clarifications

### What MediaKeepa Does
- MediaKeepa is a **tool** that allows users to download publicly accessible media from the internet
- We do **NOT host** copyrighted content on our servers
- We do **NOT pre-download** or cache content for users
- Downloads are processed **on-demand** and temporarily stored (deleted after 1 hour)
- We rely on yt-dlp, an open-source tool used by millions worldwide

### User Responsibility
- Users are responsible for ensuring their use of downloaded content complies with applicable laws
- Our Terms of Service prohibit illegal use of the service
- We do not encourage or facilitate copyright infringement

### DMCA Safe Harbor
MediaKeepa operates under DMCA Safe Harbor provisions (17 U.S.C. § 512(c)). We:
- Do not have actual knowledge of infringing activity
- Do not receive financial benefit directly attributable to infringing activity
- Respond expeditiously to remove or disable access to allegedly infringing material upon notification

## Counter-Notification Procedure

If you believe your content was wrongly removed due to a DMCA notice, you may file a counter-notification with:

1. **Your contact information** (name, address, email, phone)
2. **Identification of the removed material** and where it appeared
3. **A statement under penalty of perjury** that you have a good faith belief the material was removed by mistake or misidentification
4. **Consent to jurisdiction** of the Federal District Court
5. **Your physical or electronic signature**

We will forward your counter-notification to the original complainant. If they do not file a lawsuit within 10-14 business days, we may restore the content.

## Repeat Infringer Policy

In accordance with the DMCA and other applicable laws, we have adopted a policy of terminating, in appropriate circumstances, users who are deemed to be repeat infringers. We may also block access to users who repeatedly violate our Terms of Service or submit URLs to infringing content.

**Three-Strike Policy:**
1. **First violation:** Warning + URL blocked
2. **Second violation:** 30-day service suspension
3. **Third violation:** Permanent ban (IP + email)

## False Claims

Please note that under 17 U.S.C. § 512(f), any person who knowingly materially misrepresents that material or activity is infringing may be subject to liability. We take false DMCA claims seriously and may pursue legal action against individuals who submit fraudulent notices.

## Platform-Specific Considerations

### YouTube

- Copyright claims should be directed to the original YouTube uploader

### TikTok, Instagram, Twitter
- We download publicly posted content only
- Users are responsible for respecting original creators' rights
- Private/protected content cannot be accessed without authorization

### DRM-Protected Platforms (Netflix, Spotify Premium, etc.)
- We do **NOT support** and **actively block** DRM-protected platforms
- No DRM circumvention technology is used
- These platforms are blocked to comply with DMCA anti-circumvention provisions (17 U.S.C. § 1201)
